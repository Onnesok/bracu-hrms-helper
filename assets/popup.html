Month settings

The target is recalculated as scheduled working days × 8 hours. October 2026 uses your confirmed 17 days; other months use a calendar estimate.

For example, 17 scheduled working days automatically means a 136-hour target. This is a per-month count, not a fixed 136-hour target for other months.

Automatic calendar settings

Choose scheduled weekdays. Automatic mode counts those dates, excludes H/W off-days seen in imported HRMS records, and applies the exceptions below. It cannot know holidays that are not yet loaded.

Regular working days (all months)

Weekend work (WP) still counts toward worked hours, but does not automatically become an official scheduled workday.

Default follows the previous attendance calculation convention. Changing this only affects future scans/imports; reimport older rows if needed.
